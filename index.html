🎁 Reward Odds

Float qualities: Factory New (0–0.07), Minimal Wear (0.07–0.15), Field-Tested (0.15–0.38), Well-Worn (0.38–0.45), Battle-Scarred (0.45–1). Floats are uniformly rolled. Wear multiplies prices from 1× (BS) to 2× (FN).

Knife reward: 2% per spin in every case. Skin rarities are rolled separately when you win a knife. StatTrak: 10% (5× value); Souvenir: 1% (20× value). Both can stack (100×).
