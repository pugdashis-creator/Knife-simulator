🎁 Reward Odds

Float qualities: Factory New (0–0.07), Minimal Wear (0.07–0.15), Field-Tested (0.15–0.38), Well-Worn (0.38–0.45), Battle-Scarred (0.45–1). Floats are uniformly rolled. Wear multiplies prices from 1× (BS) to 2× (FN).

Knife reward: 2% per spin in standard cases (the Diamond Case has no knives; the 50/50 Case has a 50% knife chance). Skin rarities are rolled separately when you win a knife. StatTrak: 10% (5× value); Souvenir: 1% (20× value). Both can stack (100×).

Merge two knives of the same upgrade tier to guarantee the next tier. Choose which knife to keep first: its skin, float, rarity, StatTrak and Souvenir stay unchanged. The second knife is consumed. No random failures.
