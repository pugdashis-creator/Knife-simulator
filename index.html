🎁 Reward Odds

Float qualities: Factory New (0–0.07), Minimal Wear (0.07–0.15), Field-Tested (0.15–0.38), Well-Worn (0.38–0.45), Battle-Scarred (0.45–1). Floats are uniformly rolled. Wear multiplies prices from 1× (BS) to 2× (FN).

Knife reward: 2% in standard cases; 5% in Elite+ cases. Skin rarities are rolled separately when you win a knife. StatTrak: 10% (5× value); Souvenir: 1% (20× value). Both can stack (100×).

⚒️ Knife Upgrader

Merge two knives with matching type, skin, tier, StatTrak and Souvenir. Their floats are averaged and wear and value recalculated. The second knife is consumed. No random failures.

Multipliers are total multipliers, not cumulative. Both knives must have the exact same knife type, skin, and upgrade tier. Wear, float, StatTrak, and Souvenir may differ; the first knife is kept.

Upgrade All merges every matching pair automatically, including newly created pairs. For each pair, the earlier knife in your inventory is kept.
